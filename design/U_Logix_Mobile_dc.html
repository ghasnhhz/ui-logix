TELEGRAM MINI APP
Calculate first. Sign up only to book.
Same tokens, same seeded rate engine and the same five wizard steps as the desktop app — re-laid out for a 402pt viewport inside Telegram. A guest can price a shipment and compare all six carriers; the account is only requested at the moment they tap Book, and Telegram identity does most of the work.
JUMP TO A SCREEN
The guest gate
Book opens a bottom sheet, not a new screen — the chosen quote stays visible above the form so the user never loses the price they picked. Telegram identity is pre-filled; only company and phone are asked.
What changed from desktop
Comparison table → cards. Benchmark sidebar → one strip. Filter rail → horizontal chips. Booking page → sheet. Left rail → three tabs, and those tabs only exist after signup.
What stayed identical
Navy/amber tokens, Plus Jakarta Sans + Plex Mono, the five wizard steps, auto freight class, the single amber best-value flag, and the CSV columns.
Still out of scope
No in-app payment, no installments, no ratings, no document vault, no live carrier API. Booking ends at a reference and an offline-settlement note.
